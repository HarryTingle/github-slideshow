# Decision records

Numbered, append-only records of decisions that would otherwise be re-argued every few weeks. Product, commercial and technical decisions all live here together — in this business they are the same conversation.

## Rules

- One decision per file: `NNNN-short-title.md`
- Append-only. To change a decision, write a new record that supersedes the old one and mark the old one `Superseded by NNNN`.
- Record the decision even when it felt obvious. The value is in the *why*, recoverable months later.

## Format

```
# NNNN — Title
Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Deciders:

## Context
What forced a decision.

## Decision
What we are doing.

## Consequences
What this makes easy, what it makes hard, what we accept.

## Alternatives considered
What we rejected and why.
```
