## Rules

- **Quote people.** Actual words, dated, attributed to a role. Paraphrase is where insight goes to die.
- **Separate observation from conclusion.** What they said, then what we think it means, clearly divided.
- **Keep the contradictions.** Evidence that cuts against the thesis is more valuable than evidence that confirms it. Never quietly drop it.
- **Pain before feature.** Record the problem in their language, not our solution in ours.
- **Redact where sensitive.** Individual names and commercially sensitive figures can be pseudonymised; roles and structures cannot.

## The question we are answering

Not "would you use this?" — everyone says yes. The questions that matter:

1. Walk me through the last complex model you built. What did you actually do?
2. What broke, and what did it cost you?
3. When the client asked for a different commercial shape, what happened next?
4. Who else needed a version of that model, and what did you have to build for them?
5. What would have to be true for you to stop using Excel for this?
