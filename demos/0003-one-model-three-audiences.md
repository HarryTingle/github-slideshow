# 0003 — One model, three audiences

Proves: M3 — shareable outputs
Specs exercised: 0004
Audience: internal, then design partners
Runtime: target 6 minutes
Status: Planned

## The setup

Scenario B has been chosen. Now three people need something: the resourcing lead needs to know who and when, the bid team needs a proposal section by tomorrow, and the SLT meets on Thursday.

## The tension

This is the point where the model normally stops being useful and the rebuilding starts — a screenshot to resourcing, slides for the client, a summary tab for the SLT. Three artefacts, three chances to diverge from the model and from each other.

## The walkthrough

| # | Action | What they see | The point |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Generate the resourcing view | Roles, FTE by week, named people, gaps marked | The resourcing team's actual question, answered |
| 2 | Export it | Excel that opens cleanly | Meets the process that already exists |
| 3 | Generate the client view | On brand, delivery approach, team shape, commercial summary | Proposal-ready, no rebuild |
| 4 | Attempt to find a cost rate or margin in it | Not present, by design | Trustworthy enough to send without a review pass |
| 5 | Generate the SLT pack | All three scenarios, contingency, downside, cash exposure, guardrail status | Everything an approver needs, including what was rejected |
| 6 | Edit the model afterwards | The issued pack is marked stale, and does not change | Snapshots, not moving targets |
| 7 | Show elapsed time | Under a minute for all three | Pain point 4, gone |

## The payoff

Step 7. Three audiences, three fit-for-purpose artefacts, one model, no Excel, under a minute.
