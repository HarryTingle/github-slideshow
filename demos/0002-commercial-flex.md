# 0002 — Commercial flex under pressure

Proves: M2 — commercial models and scenarios
Specs exercised: 0003
Audience: internal, then design partners
Runtime: target 8 minutes
Status: Planned

**This is the demo that matters.** If it lands, the product works.

## The setup

The delivery plan from demo 0001 is finished and agreed. The bid is due Friday. On a call, the client says: *"We like the approach, but our board won't sign an open-ended T&M engagement. What does this look like fixed price? And could some of the fee be tied to the value we actually get?"*

## The tension

This question is the reason models get rebuilt at midnight. The delivery plan took two days. The commercial answer is needed before the call ends — and it has to be defensible on Monday when the SLT reads it.

## The walkthrough

| # | Action | What they see | The point |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Show the agreed plan and T&M baseline | £198k revenue, 56.4% margin | The starting position |
| 2 | Fork a scenario, set fixed price at £180k, 15% contingency | Margin 44.8%, break-even overrun 108% | *"We can absorb a doubling of effort before this loses money."* |
| 3 | Fork again: £150k base plus 10% of measured benefit | Expected 47.7%, **downside 33.8%** | The number nobody computes in the room |
| 4 | Guardrail flags scenario C on the downside | Breach shown with metric, threshold, actual, approver | The tool as a control, not just a calculator |
| 5 | Apply a 2-week slip to all three scenarios | Comparison updates live; fixed price absorbs it, T&M does not | Sensitivity in seconds |
| 6 | Show all three side by side | One screen, one comparison | What goes to the client and the SLT |
| 7 | Trace the effective day rate on scenario B | Back to assignments and rates | Defensible on Monday |

## The payoff

Three defensible commercial options, produced during the call, with the downside case visible on the one that looked most attractive. **The delivery plan was never touched.**
