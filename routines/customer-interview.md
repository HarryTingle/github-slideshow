# Routine — Customer interview

**Trigger:** before and after a conversation with a Head of Consulting or Commercial.
**Runtime:** 20 minutes prep, 45 minutes conversation, 30 minutes write-up.
**Output:** a file in `customers/interviews/`; updated hypotheses in `customers/icp.md`.

## Before

1. Read `customers/icp.md` — which hypotheses could this conversation test?
2. Read any existing account file for this practice.
3. Pick the three questions that would most change our thinking. Not eight.
4. Decide what artefact you will ask for: a redacted model, a rate card, a proposal template. **Ask for it. Artefacts beat opinions.**

## During

Use the spine in `customers/interviews/README.md`.

- **Do not demo.** A demo converts an interview into a reaction.
- Ask about the last time they did the thing, not what they generally do.
- When they describe a workaround, slow down. Workarounds people no longer notice are the product's best evidence.
- Capture their words. Their phrasing is more useful than your summary — and often better than our own positioning language.
- Chase the sigh. The moment their tone changes is the moment worth ten minutes.

## After

1. Write the interview file within 24 hours, using `_template-interview.md`. Quotes first, interpretation second, clearly separated.
2. Update the hypothesis table in `icp.md` — supported, contradicted, or still untested.
3. **Record what contradicted us.** Evidence against the thesis is the reason to do this at all.
4. Update or create the account file.
5. File any artefacts obtained into `context/inputs/` and queue `model-intake.md`.
6. If anything they said should change the roadmap, raise it in `REVIEW.md` rather than quietly editing `ROADMAP.md`.

## How you know it worked

At least one hypothesis in `icp.md` moved, and you can quote something they said that you did not expect.
